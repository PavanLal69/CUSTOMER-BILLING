# Northstar Labs Expense and Travel Policy

> Owner: Finance  
> Last reviewed: 20 September 2026

## 1. Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be reasonable, directly
related to company work, and supported by an itemized receipt. When in doubt, ask
your manager **before** spending.

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Total expense | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to INR 25,000 | People manager |
| INR 25,001 to INR 100,000 | Department head |
| Above INR 100,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Approval must be obtained before booking travel or purchasing equipment. Splitting
a purchase to stay below an approval threshold is prohibited.

## 3. Submitting expenses

- Submit expenses through **Expensify** within **30 calendar days** of the
  transaction date.
- Every line item needs an **itemized receipt**. A credit-card statement alone is
  not sufficient.
- Include the business purpose and the customer, project, or cost centre.
- Late submissions may be rejected. Finance may approve an exception for travel
  delays or medical emergencies.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after approval, usually
  within **10 business days**.

## 4. Meals

- Domestic travel meal limit: **INR 1,500 per person per day**.
- International travel meal limit: **INR 3,000 per person per day** (or local
  equivalent).
- Client meals above **INR 5,000** require manager approval before the meal.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of a pre-approved customer event
  and the approval is attached to the claim.
- Room service follows the same daily meal limit.

## 5. Hotels

- Domestic hotel limit: **INR 8,000 per night** before taxes.
- International hotel limit: **USD 200 per night** before taxes.
- Choose a hotel within a reasonable distance of the meeting or office.
- Exceptions for conference venues, safety concerns, or peak events require
  department-head approval before booking.
- Cancel unused nights; no-shows are not reimbursable without a documented reason.

## 6. Air travel

- Book **economy class** for all flights.
- **Premium economy** is allowed for flights longer than **six hours** with
  manager approval.
- **Business class** requires Vice President approval and is normally reserved for
  flights longer than nine hours or medical accommodation.
- Book through the company travel portal at least **14 days** in advance when
  possible. Rebook only for business needs or documented disruptions.
- Personal upgrades, lounge access, and seat-selection fees are not reimbursable
  unless required for a medical reason.

## 7. Ground transport

- Use the most economical reasonable option: metro, shuttle, rideshare, or taxi.
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicles is **INR 12 per kilometre**.
- Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.
- Rental cars require manager approval and must be compact or mid-size unless a
  larger vehicle is needed for equipment or a group.

## 8. Corporate cards

- Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Never use a corporate card for
  personal purchases, even if you intend to repay the amount.
- Report a lost or stolen corporate card to Finance and the card issuer
  immediately.
- Card statements must be reconciled in Expensify by the **5th of the following
  month**.

## 9. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, spa or gym fees,
airline loyalty programmes, traffic fines, lost luggage without a carrier report,
and expenses for family members or travel companions. Exceptions require CFO
approval.

## 10. Travel safety and insurance

- Register international trips in the **Travel Safety Portal** at least **five
  business days** before departure.
- Company travel insurance covers business travel, including medical emergencies.
  The policy number and emergency contact are in the portal.
- Carry a company laptop only when necessary. Follow the Information Security
  Policy for device encryption, VPN use, and public Wi-Fi.
- If a trip is disrupted, prioritize personal safety, contact the travel desk, and
  keep receipts for rebooking or accommodation.

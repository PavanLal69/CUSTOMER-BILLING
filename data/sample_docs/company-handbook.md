# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

> Owner: People Operations  
> Last reviewed: 15 September 2026

## 1. About Northstar Labs

Northstar Labs builds **Orbit**, a customer-support platform used by growing
support teams. We are a remote-first company with hubs in Bengaluru and Pune.
Our operating principles are: customer obsession, clear writing, small reversible
decisions, and ownership without hand-offs.

## 2. Working hours and remote work

- Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time (IST)**.
  Outside those hours, employees may arrange their day flexibly.
- Employees may work remotely up to **three days per week** without additional
  approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a people manager and
  People Operations.
- Teams must publish their working hours and escalation contacts in their team
  handbook.
- Employees who work from a hub city are expected to attend team anchor days,
  usually one day per week, when announced by their manager.

## 3. Paid time off (PTO)

- Eligible employees receive **20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year**.
  PTO accrues at **1.67 days per month**.
- Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next
  calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March.
- Requests for **more than three consecutive days** must be submitted at least
  **two weeks in advance** through the People Portal and approved by the manager.
- PTO requests are approved unless they conflict with a critical release or an
  already-approved teammate absence. Managers must respond within two business
  days.
- PTO does not include public holidays, sick leave, or parental leave.

## 4. Sick leave and wellbeing

- Employees receive **10 paid sick-leave days per calendar year**, separate from
  PTO.
- Notify your manager by **10:00 IST** on the first day of absence. No medical
  certificate is required for absences of three days or fewer.
- For absences longer than three days, upload a medical certificate to the People
  Portal.
- Northstar provides confidential counselling through the Employee Assistance
  Programme (EAP). Sessions are free and are not visible to managers.

## 5. Parental leave

- The **primary caregiver** is entitled to **16 weeks of fully paid parental
  leave**. The **secondary caregiver** is entitled to **6 weeks of fully paid
  leave**.
- The policy applies equally to birth, adoption, and surrogacy.
- Employees must notify People Operations at least **30 days before the intended
  start date** where practicable.
- Parental leave may be taken in up to three blocks within the first 12 months
  after the child arrives. Each block must be at least two weeks.

## 6. Public holidays

Northstar observes **12 public holidays** each year. The holiday calendar is
published in the People Portal every December for the following year. Employees
who are required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off.

## 7. Equipment and home office

- Every employee receives a company laptop and a one-time **INR 40,000 home-office
  allowance** for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment.
- The allowance must be claimed within **90 days of joining** and is processed
  through the expense system.
- Equipment remains company property and must be returned within **10 business
  days** of the last working day.
- Replacement or repair requests go through the IT Service Desk. Do not buy a
  replacement laptop without IT approval.

## 8. Learning and development

- Each employee has an annual **INR 60,000 learning budget** for courses,
  certifications, books, and conferences.
- Requests above INR 20,000 require manager approval before booking.
- Learning time of up to **four hours per month** may be used during core hours,
  with manager awareness.
- The learning budget cannot be converted to cash and does not roll over.

## 9. Performance and career reviews

- Formal performance reviews happen **twice a year**, in **April and October**.
- Reviews combine self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager assessment.
- Promotion cycles run alongside the October review. Employees must have been in
  their current role for at least **six months** before being nominated.
- Continuous feedback is encouraged; there is no requirement to save feedback
  only for the formal cycle.

## 10. Travel and expenses

Business travel must be approved before booking. The detailed limits, receipt
requirements, and reimbursement timelines are in the **Expense and Travel
Policy**. Security requirements for travel devices are in the **Information
Security Policy**.
